RESEARCH TEAM

The Visual Systems and Computer Graphics (VSCG) research group focuses on advancing research in Computer Vision, Computer Graphics, Multimedia, and Intelligent Visual Computing. Our work emphasizes the development of innovative AI-driven methods for visual content analysis, understanding, generation, and enhancement. By bridging theoretical foundations with practical applications, we aim to address emerging challenges in visual computing and explore interdisciplinary research opportunities. Through collaborative research and hands-on projects, VSCG provides a supportive environment for students to develop their research skills, foster creativity, and pursue academic and professional growth.
